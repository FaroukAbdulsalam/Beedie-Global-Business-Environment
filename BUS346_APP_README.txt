BUS 346 — Global Expansion Decision Lab

WHAT IT DOES
The app turns BUS 346 into one integrated management decision:
1. Identify Opportunities — Globalization, Economic Development, Trade Theory
2. Assess Risk — Political/Legal, Culture, Ethics/CSR, Trade Policy, Regional Integration, FX, Monetary System
3. Choose Entry Strategy — FDI, International Strategy, Organization, Developed/Emerging Market Entry
4. Boardroom Decision — students defend a final recommendation

HOW TO RUN
1. Install Python 3.10+
2. In Terminal / Command Prompt:
   pip install streamlit pandas numpy
3. Run:
   streamlit run bus346_global_expansion_lab.py

TEACHING DESIGN
- Students choose a company, industry, and candidate markets.
- Opportunity scores respond to strategic weights.
- The instructor can introduce shocks (tariffs, FX depreciation, political instability, cultural backlash, integration).
- Students choose entry-mode priorities and receive a heuristic recommendation.
- Final board pitch forces them to defend the decision rather than simply accept a score.

IMPORTANT
The country values are illustrative demo values for teaching. They should not be presented as live or official country-risk data.
